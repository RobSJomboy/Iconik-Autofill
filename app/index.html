Push to Iconik

Jomboy Media's Mac app for editors, producers and socials. Export, double-click, done. It uploads your export to the show's Iconik folder, waits for the video on YouTube, then fills the Iconik metadata and the monday ad reads.

Install (one time)

  1. Open Terminal (press ⌘ Space, type “Terminal”, press Return).
  2. Paste this line and press Return:
curl -fsSL https://robsjomboy.github.io/Iconik-Autofill/app/install.sh | bash
  1. The app opens by itself, and there's a Push to Iconik icon on your Desktop. Follow the setup. You'll need the team key from Rob Scirocco.
Why Terminal? The app isn't from the App Store, so a browser download gets blocked by macOS. Installing this way skips that. Updates show up as an Update button inside the app.

Every day

  1. Export like you always do.
  2. Double-click Push to Iconik. Your newest export uploads to the right show.
  3. Close the window if you want. When the video is live on YouTube, the metadata fills itself and you get a notification.

Requires macOS 12 or newer, on Apple Silicon or Intel. Iconik Autofill is the web version.
