How it works
  1. Paste the published YouTube link and hit Generate.
  2. Title, date, link, episode ID and episode # come straight from YouTube.
  3. Sponsor is matched against the description. Tags, type and the one-sentence description are AI picks, so give them a look.
  4. Hit Push to Iconik. It finds the asset by title (or paste its Iconik link) and fills every field. Copy buttons still work if you'd rather paste by hand.
